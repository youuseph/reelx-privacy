ReelX privacy policy

Last updated: October 31, 2026

ReelX is a Chrome extension for viewing and adjusting photos and videos on the pages you visit.

Data we collect

None. ReelX does not collect, store or transmit your photos, videos, browsing history or personal data. There are no accounts, analytics or ads.

How processing works

Photos and videos are processed locally on your computer. The AI models are part of the extension and do not contact any server. On x.com, ReelX reads the author, caption and counts of a post from the page only to show them in the viewer. That information never leaves your browser.

Permissions

Access to all sites is needed so you can use ReelX on any website. It works only on the photo or video you choose.

External links
